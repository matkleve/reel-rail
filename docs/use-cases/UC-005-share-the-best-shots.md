# UC-005 — Share the best shots

<!-- id: UC-005 -->
<!-- built-in: src/store/picks.ts, src/series, src/fs/share.ts -->

**Who:** someone who wants to post, send or print a selection.
**Wants to:** collect the keepers as they go and hand them on in one step.
**So that:** posting is not a second sorting session.

## Success looks like

- Space / Pick marks an item; on a computer it is copied into a picks
  folder, on a phone "Share picks" opens the share sheet.
- A selection becomes a named series, exported or shared numbered `01_…`.
