# 2026-11-12 — German UI

- **User asked for the app in German** (for their brother's German iPhone).
  Copy stays English in the code, wrapped in `t()`; `src/i18n` picks German
  when the device's first language is German and looks the English text up in
  `src/i18n/de*.ts`. English everywhere else, so the e2e browsers (en-US) are
  unchanged.
  - Informal "du". Picks are **Auswahl** (Auswählen / ausgewählt); a
    multi-selection is **Markierung** (markiert) so the two never share a word.
    "Recently deleted" is **Zuletzt gelöscht**, as in the Photos app.
  - Plurals are separate keys (`tn(one, other, n)`), which also fixed English
    "Delete 1 files" / "1 items".
  - `i18n.test.ts` scans `src/` for `t("…")` / `tn("…", "…")` and fails on a
    missing, unused or placeholder-mismatched key, and on a non-literal key.
  - Dates use `locale` (de-DE / en-GB). New libraries get German tag presets;
    stored presets are left alone. Series titles' word lists stay English.
  - iOS: `CFBundleLocalizations` (en, de) in Info.plist, or WKWebView reports
    English on a German phone (TRAPS). The Photos permission prompt is still
    English.
  - Shortcut list: ↑/↓ no longer say "bigger and higher" (the strip is flat
    since 2026-11-11); the four throws in Sort are listed.
  - German is longer: the phone's hint pill now wraps between hints (never
    inside one), and a day label at the timeline's right end is pulled in
    instead of cut off ("Do., 15. Okt.").

## Several clips out of one video (UC-007)

The user: "was wenn ich mehrere Snippets rausschneiden will aus einem Video?
kannst du dafür Use Cases machen und es ausprogrammieren". The trim screen
now holds a list of clips; one clip without "keep the whole video" is the old
plain trim (replaces the file), anything else writes each clip as its own
video (`-clip1`, `-clip2` in video order) and rates the original *Delete*
unless kept. A folder left as it is keeps one remembered trim — it may not
write files — so "Add clip" is hidden there.

Found on the way: clips seconds apart were folded into a *burst* and only
one showed. Videos are no longer stacked at all — a burst means "same moment,
pick one", which is never what two videos are.

Phone: dialog footer notes now take their own line (they were squeezed into
a column of single words beside the buttons), and the trim video is shorter
so the clip controls stay in view.

## The deck, on a real iPhone

The user's screenshots from Safari: the frame did not fit the photo (blurred
side bars, black corners), the drag stuttered, the card seemed to vanish on
release, a "2" on a card (a burst) made no sense, and the "Good · Undo" pill
over the photo matched nothing.

- The print is cut to the photo's shape (`--aspect` from the preview, sized
  with container units) — no blur, no black.
- Drag writes CSS variables once per frame (useDeckDrag); React renders only
  when a drag starts and ends.
- The fly-out continues along the throw from where the finger let go
  (flyOut.ts), 520 ms, an ease that keeps its speed.
- The deck deals every photo; bursts fold only on the timeline.
- The next card is invisible until the top one moves: at rest one print, not
  a pile.
- The note sits under the buttons with the verdict's icon and colour.
- 100 detail use cases in docs/use-cases/DETAILS.md; next up: the timeline as
  day stacks that fan out (D-031).

## Timeline as day stacks (phone)

The user: "jeder Tag einen Stapel … wenn ich draufklicke muss der
auffächern". On the phone the Timeline tab now shows one stack per day (best
photo on top, two peeking out, "5 photos · ★ 2 · 3 open"). A tap fans the day
out into the strip — every photo, bursts unfolded — with a day-relative count
and "All days" to fold it back; swiping past the day's last photo runs on into
the next day. iPad and desktop keep the flat strip (keyboard users, and the
desktop e2e walks it). Bars still show the whole trip.

## Stack by: Single · Day · Time · Place

The user: "wenn man in dieser Ansicht die Stapel-Art verändern kann … jedes
Bild einzeln oder nach Ort oder nach Zeit (Zeitgruppen mathematisch) oder
nach Tag". A switch above the stacks (remembered per device):
- Single: no stacks, the strip of every photo (bursts unfolded).
- Day: calendar days.
- Time: split at gaps that stand out — log-scale median + 3 MAD of the
  trip's own gaps, clamped to 30 min … 6 h (src/timeline/groups.ts). Median
  and MAD rather than mean and SD: the few big gaps we look for must not set
  the bar themselves (with mean/SD a 2½-hour lunch did not split).
- Place: typed place name, else GPS within 1.5 km of a place's middle
  (revisits join it), else "No place".
← / → walk the groups one after another (`nav` in the store), so a place
group — not one stretch of time — still runs on into the next one.

Then: "ich würde auch bei den Stapeln trotzdem gerne links und rechts
scrollen, warum ist das jetzt eine Liste" — the stacks are a horizontal,
snapping strip now (the middle one moves the bars' marker), and "Stack up"
slides the photos back into the middle before the stacks return.
