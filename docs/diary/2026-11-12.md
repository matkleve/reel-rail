# 2026-11-12 — German UI

- **User asked for the app in German** (for their brother's German iPhone).
  Copy stays English in the code, wrapped in `t()`; `src/i18n` picks German
  when the device's first language is German and looks the English text up in
  `src/i18n/de*.ts`. English everywhere else, so the e2e browsers (en-US) are
  unchanged.
  - Informal "du". Picks are **Auswahl** (Auswählen / ausgewählt); a
    multi-selection is **Markierung** (markiert) so the two never share a word.
    "Recently deleted" is **Zuletzt gelöscht**, as in the Photos app.
  - Plurals are separate keys (`tn(one, other, n)`), which also fixed English
    "Delete 1 files" / "1 items".
  - `i18n.test.ts` scans `src/` for `t("…")` / `tn("…", "…")` and fails on a
    missing, unused or placeholder-mismatched key, and on a non-literal key.
  - Dates use `locale` (de-DE / en-GB). New libraries get German tag presets;
    stored presets are left alone. Series titles' word lists stay English.
  - iOS: `CFBundleLocalizations` (en, de) in Info.plist, or WKWebView reports
    English on a German phone (TRAPS). The Photos permission prompt is still
    English.
  - Shortcut list: ↑/↓ no longer say "bigger and higher" (the strip is flat
    since 2026-11-11); the four throws in Sort are listed.
  - German is longer: the phone's hint pill now wraps between hints (never
    inside one), and a day label at the timeline's right end is pulled in
    instead of cut off ("Do., 15. Okt.").

## Timeline buttons = deck buttons

The user: "We have four states now, but in the timeline the buttons are
different again." The dock showed five levels (bin, thumbs down, circle,
thumbs up, star). It now shows the deck's four verdicts with the deck's icons
and order (bin, ✕, ★, ♥); tapping the lit one again takes the rating back to
"not rated", which has no button — it is the starting state, not a verdict.
Also: start-screen copy, the deck hint under the buttons, and a taller phone
timeline with legend (5e6b620).
