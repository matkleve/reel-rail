# 2026-11-11 — Swipe deck on the phone, Photos source, iPhone build without a Mac

- **User asked for a Tinder-like look** for the whole app, keeping the
  features (trimming etc.). A clickable mock-up was shown first and approved
  ("passt perfekt"). Built for the **phone only** (≤ 600 px): there the
  carousel, dock and stage overlay give way to `src/swipe/` — one card,
  thrown right = Good, left = Discard, up = Favorite, down = Not great; the
  deck deals the next one by itself. Round buttons for the same throws, ↺,
  and scissors (video) / tags (photo). Header and waveform stay.
  - Larger screens keep the carousel: with a keyboard and a wide rail it is
    faster, and the swipe axes would fight the arrow keys.
  - The deck's axes are the other way round from the carousel's (sideways
    rates instead of browsing). Browsing back and forth is the waveform's job
    on the phone. Hold-to-select is not on the deck yet — series are made on
    a bigger screen for now.
  - The deck keeps its own undo history: a throw that does not change the
    rating pushes nothing onto the store's undo stack, so ↺ would otherwise
    take back an older, unrelated decision (`src/swipe/deck.ts`).
  - Chrome stays neutral (design system rule 1). The mock-up's pink/orange
    Tinder gradient was offered and not taken; the four rating colours carry
    the vibe (stamps, buttons).
- **Native app identity decided** (was the open question in NATIVE.md): in
  the app an item is keyed by its Photos id (`ph-…`), not the content hash.
  Hashing needs every original copied out first. The app is the Free tier
  (no rating sync), so the cost — the same photo on the computer is another
  item — is zero today. `SourceFile.known` lets a source skip reading.
- `PhotosSource` is read-only (`kind: "files"`): discards are hidden, never
  deleted from Photos. Deleting into Recently Deleted needs its own
  edit-or-leave choice first (hard rule 1).
- **User has no Mac**, a Windows PC and (probably) a private repo. Added
  `.github/workflows/ios.yml`: an unsigned .ipa on GitHub's macOS runners,
  started by hand or by an `ios-*` tag (macOS minutes count ×10 on a private
  repo). Installed with Sideloadly and a free Apple ID: runs 7 days, then
  sideload again. TestFlight (99 €/year) is the step after.
- The user asked to work on `main`; the remote had no `main` (default branch
  was `claude/adoring-mendel-2ngvsc`), so `main` was created from it.
- e2e (folder mode, desktop) fails at the Picks step in the cloud session's
  Chromium — also on the commit before these changes, so it is the browser
  version there, not this change. CI is the judge.
